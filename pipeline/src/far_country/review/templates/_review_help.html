What am I approving?

Each row is a single descriptor — an atomic claim about heaven, grounded in the cited verse(s), classified by a curator-assigned tier, and (when symbolic) pointed at what the symbol refers to. Approving the row affirms all four interpretive claims at once:

  1. The statement is a true claim about heaven grounded in the cited verse(s).
  2. The tier classification is correct (clear / fuzzy / debated / symbolic).
  3. The symbolic_referent (when tier is symbolic) accurately captures what the symbol points to — not just a restatement of the surface image.
  4. The citation (book, chapter, verses) is correct.

If you agree with most of a row but disagree on one claim, use Edit — don't reject. Decision triage:

  • Approve — all four claims look right.
  • Edit — claim is fine but tier, referent, statement wording, or citation needs adjusting. Edit returns the row to pending for your re-approval.
  • Reject — the descriptor doesn't belong in the dataset at all. Use for hallucinations (verse doesn't say this), paraphrase-bloat that adds nothing the verse doesn't say, duplicates, or claims that aren't really about heaven. Requires reviewer notes.
  • Discuss — you want a second pass on a theological judgment before deciding. Parks the row for batched follow-up.

Keyboard: a approve · r reject · d discuss · e edit. Approval feeds the canonical dataset that grounds Phase 2 Q&A and (later) Phase 3's 3D world. Symbolic-vs-literal rendering decisions are downstream policy (ADR 0009 / 0010) — they don't belong in this judgment.
