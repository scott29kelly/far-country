## Recurring directives (the set speaks with one voice)

1. **Tier faces are translucent gold GLASS, lit from within, with readable
   interiors** — fine window lattices (whole facades of small arched panes)
   framed by repeated ARCADE courses at every setback, with pale IVORY/WHITE
   cornice bands alternating against the gold (not gold-on-gold). The
   M3 material pass should treat each tier face as: giant arch bays →
   mullion grids → glass with interior-glow gradient + parallax depth.
   (new-jerusalem-1/-2, mount-sinai-2, width/height comparisons,
   millenial-life-1.)
2. **The plateau EDGE is stratified cliff with waterfalls pouring off the
   rim** — every aerial shows a mesa-edged allotment with layered rock walls
   and multiple falls (holy-allotment, new-jerusalem-1, gemini-render-1).
   Terrain follow-up: steepen the ADR 0015 rim falloff into a cliff-and-
   talus band and let plateau streams spill over it. The TOP stays gently
   rolling green (matches Willis text: "gently rolling… scattered trees and
   meadow").
3. **The plain is ordered patchwork right up to the built zones** — field
   grids, orchard rows, hedgerows, neighborhood bands (Levite / Zadok), a
   perimeter greenbelt. This is the zone-map milestone's blueprint
   (holy-allotment, holy-allotment-layout, new-jerusalem-2,
   millenial-life-2's Tuscan vineyards/olives for the crop texture).
4. **The river of life** — a crystal-clear teal THREAD over a cobbled bed,
   flowering banks, PALM allées flanking it; its source is a rainbow-haloed
   radiant fountain (Rev 4:3 — RENDERING-DECISIONS #4's halo) with twin
   falls and mist (river-of-life-1/-3).
5. **The temple is architecturally alien to the city** — a fortified
   red-sandstone Ezekiel compound (crenellated walls, corner/gate towers,
   cloistered courts, glowing sanctuary) with the Ezek 47 river issuing
   from UNDER its wall through arched culverts, pilgrim torch-lines
   converging (temple-complex-wide/-medium). Delta #8 answered; the wide
   shot also models the city-base arcade behind it.
6. **The world is inhabited and ordinary-scaled at its feet** — children
   playing by streams, elders on benches (Zech 8:4-5), villages and towns
   coexisting under the megastructure, rooftop gardens, sky-bridges,
   carved-wood gilded SIGNPOSTS ("TEMPLE / ZADOKSBURG / LEVITESVILLE") for
   wayfinding voice (millenial-life-1/-4/-5). Feeds M3.4 wayfinding + M4
   population.
7. **Holiness gradient** — Sinai prototype → city fulfillment: Presence at
   the summit (cloud/fire → glory), restricted-access courses at the base,
   priests' zone, ordered camp/dwelling grid (mount-sinai-1/-2).
8. **Scale mood** — 11-mi city dwarfing Everest/Burj/Chicago (height/width
   comparisons, ezekiel's-holy-allotment-blueprint: the city footprint as
   1/5 of the allotment). We deliberately run ~2.5 mi (ADR 0014); these are
   intent, not our metric. Bruegel's Babel is the cultural-memory contrast
   (a spiral under construction — what our city is NOT: it descends whole).
