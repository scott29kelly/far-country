# Willis's New Jerusalem model (research notes)

Working notes distilled from Janet Willis's published articles and her book
*What on Earth Is Heaven Like? A Look at God's City: New Jerusalem* (3rd ed.,
2015; rewritten 2024/25 as *...The New Creation and the New Jerusalem*). These
are **her claims, with attribution** — not reproductions of her text. Per
[ADR 0006](../adr/0006-source-licensing-posture.md) we do not redistribute her
prose; we record her positions and reasoning so the dataset and the 3D world
can defer to her where the project chooses to.

> **Translation note.** Willis quotes the **NASB**, not the ESV. Before any of
> these claims enter the canonical dataset, re-verify each citation against the
> ESV ([non-negotiable #4](../../CLAUDE.md)).

---

## Who she is, and why it matters

Janet Willis (wife of Chicago Bible-church pastor Scott Willis) lost six of her
nine children in a 1994 highway accident. Her study of heaven grew out of that
grief; the book's tone is pastoral and consolatory as well as analytic — heaven
studied as the concrete place where she expects bodily reunion with her
children. This frames her instinct toward a **literal, physical, located,
inhabited** heaven over an abstract or purely spiritual one.

Sources: [scottandjanetwillis.com/about-janet](http://scottandjanetwillis.com/about-janet.html),
[CBS Chicago](https://www.cbsnews.com/chicago/news/deadly-tragedy-spurs-janet-willis-to-write-book-on-heaven/).

## Interpretive method

- **Comparing Scripture with Scripture / harmonizing.** Her signature move is
  identifying **Ezekiel's city (Ezek 40–48) with John's New Jerusalem (Rev
  21–22)** as the *same* structure, then reading their measurements and features
  as mutually interpreting.
- **Literal about the text, revisionist about the conventional arithmetic** (see
  size, below). She resists allegorizing the architectural details.
- **Typological + archaeological streak** beyond standard grammatical-historical
  exegesis: the worldwide distribution of pyramids/ziggurats is offered as a
  cultural memory of God's mountain dwelling (her most speculative chain, which
  she flags as speculative).

## Shape — a step pyramid / mountain (NOT a cube)

Her central architectural thesis. Against the common cube reading (drawn from
the Holy of Holies, 1 Kgs 6:20), she argues the city is **mountain- /
pyramid-shaped**, specifically a **step pyramid (ziggurat)**:

- God's dwelling is repeatedly "the holy mountain of God" (Zech 8:3; Isa 2:2;
  Ps 48:1–2; 68:15–16; Ex 15:17–18). Isa 2:2 ("the mountain... on the chief of
  the mountains") → "a mountain on top of a mountain" = a peaked structure on a
  plateau.
- **Stepped specifically:** Amos 9:6 "builds his upper chambers (*maʿalah*)" —
  *maʿalah* usually = "stairway/steps" (cf. the Songs of Ascents/Steps, Ps
  120–134); Ps 122:3 "compact together" (*chabar* = couple/heap up/join) →
  "each level laid upon another... a step pyramid."
- **El Shaddai ≈ "God of the mountain"** (Akkadian *šadû*, "mountain") — offered
  as supporting evidence (contested etymology; she acknowledges "Almighty" is
  the usual gloss).
- **Eden / Mountain of God** (Ezek 28:11–19): Eden sat on "the holy mountain of
  God" with "stones of fire"; she infers the New Jerusalem is the eschatological
  Mountain of God / restored Eden.
- **Sinai type** (Heb 12:18–29): five parallels between the Sinai camp and
  Ezekiel's Holy Allotment — restricted mountain access, priest/Levite
  residences around it, the worship structure's position, cloud-and-fire, and
  God's presence at the top.
- Prior scholars she leans on: **Alva McClain** ("rising... by the great
  step-backs of its twelve-jeweled foundations") and **Paul Enns** ("a cone or
  pyramid, with God's throne at the top").

She presents the *shape* as argued/likely (hedged "plausible conception"), and
has produced **her own artwork** of it.

## Size — the cubic reading of Rev 21:16

Her signature, contrarian claim:

- Mainstream: 12,000 stadia **linear** ≈ 1,380–1,500 mi per side (Alcorn, Price,
  MacArthur "difficult to envision").
- **Willis:** 12,000 stadia is the **area of the (square) base.** √12,000 ≈ 110
  stadia per side; ×~0.11 mi ≈ **~12 miles per side**, and height ≈ base, so
  **~12 mi × 12 mi × 12 mi bounding** filled as a steep pyramid (~63° faces; she
  gives no explicit angle). She argues first-century readers, schooled in
  Babylonian/Greek square-root methods, would read a "squared" figure.
- The **"1,331 cubic miles" number circulating online is NOT hers** — she states
  ~12 mi/side and does not give a cubic-mileage total.
- **144-cubit wall** (Rev 21:17) = ~72 yards ≈ **216 ft** — the perimeter wall
  of the **base course**, short relative to the ~12-mi-high mountain (expected,
  not a problem).
- **Compatibility with Ezekiel:** Ezekiel's city = 4,500 rods + 250-rod border
  per side = 5,000 rods ≈ **9.94–11.36 mi/side** (rod = 10.5–12 ft) — "about 11
  miles," compatible with John's ~12. Dead Sea Scroll *New Jerusalem* text
  (4Q554): "100 stades" ≈ 11 mi — corroborating.

## Levels, foundations, throne

- **Twelve foundations = the stepped courses.** *themelios* = "the horizontal
  part of a structure... not necessarily equal in size or height," so the twelve
  gem foundations (Rev 21:14, 19–20) become **decreasing-width terraces**, the
  "great step-backs." She does not lock the visible terrace count to twelve, but
  twelve is the natural mapping.
- **Throne / glory at the summit** (apex), per the mountain model and Enns; the
  glory of God is the light there (Rev 21:23; Isa 60:19–20).
- **Gates + wall at the base:** twelve gates, three per side, named for the
  tribes, perpetually open, an angel at each (Rev 21:12–13, 25). She follows
  **Ezekiel 48:30–34** gate ordering, harmonizing to Ezekiel.

## Materials, light, atmosphere

- **Transparent / crystalline.** Rev 21:11 "brilliance... like crystal-clear
  jasper"; Rev 21:18,21 gold "like clear glass." She invokes **diamond**
  (hardest, highest compressive strength) to answer how an 11-mi structure could
  stand — i.e. the city is a clear, jewel-like, self-luminous mass.
- **Twelve gem foundations** (Rev 21:19–20), tied to Isa 54:11–13 (foundations
  of sapphire, gates of crystal, walls of precious stones) and Ezek 28:13
  ("stones of fire").
- **Glory is the light** — no sun/moon needed (Rev 21:23; 22:5; 21:25 "no
  night"). A huge enclosed structure "needs its own light source."
- **Cloud-and-fire canopy** over the summit (Isa 4:5–6; cf. Sinai, Ex 19:18;
  24:15–17).

## Location, cosmology, "Father's house"

- A **literal, physical, earthly** city that **descends from heaven onto the
  renewed earth** (Rev 21:2,10), located on **Ezekiel's Holy Allotment in
  restored Israel**, on an elevated plateau.
- **City and temple are distinct, adjacent** ("Washington D.C." analogy): a
  temple + priestly-residence district near, but separate from, the city — and
  **no temple *inside* the city** (Rev 21:22; Ezek 48:10).
- **"The Father's house" (John 14:2) IS the New Jerusalem itself** — its "many
  rooms" are **residences in/on the city** — explicitly **NOT** the Ezekiel
  temple (she argues against that identification).
- **Renewal over annihilation** for "new heaven and new earth" (continuity of
  the physical creation).
- **Inhabitants are embodied and active** — resurrected saints "going in and out
  on special assignments"; a governing capital, not static bliss; nations/kings
  bring their glory through the open gates (Rev 21:24–26).

## Eschatology — adopted in full (ADR 0012)

Willis is **premillennial (pre-wrath)**; her publisher (Alan Kurschner /
Eschatos Ministries) brands her view **"Premillennial New Creationism."**
Distinctives:

- A **literal future millennium**, with the New Jerusalem descending at its
  **beginning** (her argument: the tree's leaves "for the healing of the
  nations," Rev 22:2, require mortal nations present — i.e. during the
  millennium), persisting "during the millennium and for all eternity."
- **Ezekiel 40–48 as a literal future millennial temple + functioning
  priesthood** in restored national Israel.
- **Renewal over annihilation** for the new heavens and new earth.
- A **pre-wrath** reading of the church's relation to the tribulation (the
  church passes through the earlier tribulation and is gathered to Christ before
  the Day of the Lord's wrath).

Endorsers (David Burggraff, Shepherds Theological Seminary; commended by Erwin
Lutzer) sit in the dispensational/premil orbit; no mainstream Reformed academic
review found.

**Project stance (2026-05-29):** Willis's eschatology is now **adopted in full**
as the project's framing. The project initially carried a Reformed amillennial
framing ([ADR 0008](../adr/0008-eschatological-framing.md)) as a *temporary
simplification* to reach the 3D world faster; that simplification has served its
purpose and is now superseded by
[**ADR 0012**](../adr/0012-eschatological-framing-premillennial.md), which locks
the project to premillennial (pre-wrath) New Creationism. There is no longer any
quarantine: Willis's framework is the project's framework. Accordingly:

- **Her ARCHITECTURE remains in `/world`** (pyramid, ~scale-deferred placeholder,
  summit throne, terraced gem foundations, crystalline materials, glory-light,
  cascading single river, Ezekiel gate order) — as before, and was always
  framework-portable.
- **Her premil-specific surroundings are now in-scope build targets, not
  quarantined material:** the literal future Ezekiel 40–48 temple + priestly
  district (distinct from and adjacent to the city), the Holy Allotment and
  tribal geography (Ezek 48), the Ezekiel 47 healing river issuing from the
  temple (distinct from the Rev 22:1 city river already built), the
  restored-national-Israel setting, and the dynamism of mortal nations making
  pilgrimage (Zech 14:16). These are organized into the Millennial-Kingdom
  milestone in [`roadmap.md`](../roadmap.md). Such claims enter the dataset under
  the normal tier system (most `clear` or `fuzzy`; genuinely contested
  sub-questions like the function of the millennial sacrifices remain `debated`)
  — no longer auto-tagged `debated` purely for being premil-specific.

See [`RENDERING-DECISIONS.md`](../../RENDERING-DECISIONS.md) entry #1 and

## Tribe → gate ordering (Ezekiel 48:30–34)

Willis harmonizes to Ezekiel's ordering, reading clockwise/by side:

- **North:** Reuben, Judah, Levi
- **East:** Joseph, Benjamin, Dan
- **South:** Simeon, Issachar, Zebulun
- **West:** Gad, Asher, Naphtali

(Note: Ezekiel's list includes **Dan** and **Joseph** — it does not use the Rev
7:5–8 substitutions of Manasseh-for-Dan. The `/world` previously used a Rev 7
illustrative order; deferring to Willis switches it to Ezekiel's.)

## Visual composition (from Willis's own artwork)

New observations from **her illustrations** in two print articles (*Biblical
Prophecy* magazine): "Why the New Jerusalem Is Shaped Like a Mountain" (Winter
2022) and "The Size of New Jerusalem" (Spring 2023). These are the print
counterparts of the bibleprophecydaily.com articles already indexed below;
"Artwork by Janet Willis." Per [ADR 0006](../adr/0006-source-licensing-posture.md)
we describe her renderings, we do not reproduce them. This section is for **art
direction** — how she composes the scene — distinct from the doctrinal claims
above.

**The hero composition (recurring across both articles):**

- The city reads as a **terraced step-pyramid / ziggurat** rising in distinct
  decreasing-width tiers to a single summit — not a smooth cone, not a cube.
  Many shallow steps (more than a dozen visible), giving a stair-stepped
  silhouette like Djoser / El Castillo (the real-world ziggurats she prints
  alongside, as cultural-memory evidence).
- It sits on **elevated green land** — a broad rise / plateau, not a flat plain
  and not a sharp peak. The land around is **verdant and gently rolling**, with
  scattered trees and meadow.
- **Water in the foreground / around the base:** a calm river or lake leads the
  eye to the city. Distant **mountain ranges** ring the horizon under a bright,
  softly clouded **daytime sky**.
- The city is **luminous** — it glows warmer and brighter than the daylit
  landscape (the glory-light reading made visible), brightest toward the summit.

**Palette she uses:**

- City: **warm golds and ivory/sand** on the lower terraces (street-of-gold /
  jasper), shifting to **pale, cool crystalline blue-green and white** higher up,
  with a bright glow at the apex. Reads as stone-and-crystal, not metallic chrome.
- Land: saturated **verdant greens**; **soft blue water**; **hazy blue-grey**
  distant mountains; warm, high-key atmosphere (gentle, not dramatic).

**Layout diagrams (not landscape art):**

- **Holy Allotment** plan (Ezek 45/48): a **square subdivided into stacked
  horizontal bands** (priests' portion with the sanctuary, Levites' portion, the
  city portion, the prince's land flanking) — the **city sits at the south end**
  of this square district. This is the *regional* layout the city sits within.
- **Ezekiel-vs-John comparison** table: both cities on elevated land, square
  base, twelve gates (3/side), etc. — the harmonization, in checklist form.
- **Height-comparison** chart dramatizing scale (the city dwarfing the Burj
  Khalifa, 2,717 ft). NB: our build uses the **deferred ~placeholder scale**
  (ADR 0009 rule 6), so this is mood/intent, not our metric.

**Build directives for the engine scene (`?scene=newjerusalem`):**

1. **Site the city on a broad rise**, not flat ground — pick/raise a plateau in
   the heightfield so the terraced city reads as a mountain-city above the land.
2. **Put water at the approach** — a river/lake between the default spawn and the
   city, leading the eye up to it (matches her foreground water, and the Rev 22
   river can flow down to meet it).
3. **Terracing:** many shallow steps to a single bright summit; lower tiers warm
   gold/ivory, upper tiers cool crystalline, **glow concentrated at the apex**
   (throne glory) — drives material zoning and an emissive summit.
4. **Surround with verdant, gently rolling vegetated land** ringed by distant
   hills — which the restored full-landscape scene now provides.
5. **Keep the daytime, high-key, softly-clouded mood** for the establishing view.
